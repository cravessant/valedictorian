# Screening Answers

Read this before answering eligibility, work authorization, salary, EEO, consent, or free-response questions.

## General Answer Rules

- Answer only from Valedictorian state, selected materials, prior user instructions, or direct user response.
- Normalize the question meaning before matching a saved answer. Similar wording is not enough when legal, compensation, authorization, or sensitive disclosure is involved.
- For yes/no eligibility questions, use exact known facts. If unknown, pause.
- For years of experience, calculate from verifiable history and do not round up to satisfy requirements.
- For skills, certifications, licenses, education, languages, clearances, and locations, answer affirmatively only when explicitly supported.
- If a required answer is missing, record `needs_user_info` as a workflow-run blocker with the exact question or fact needed. Report that the current CLI cannot persist the corresponding Action Queue hold.

## Work Authorization And Sponsorship

- Use only explicit user-provided or Valedictorian-recorded facts.
- Do not infer citizenship, visa status, permanent residency, current work authorization, future sponsorship needs, export-control eligibility, E-Verify status, or clearance.
- Never choose a misleading answer to pass a knockout screen.
- Pause for country-specific legal status, visa type, expiration date, future sponsorship, export-control, or clearance questions unless exact approved data exists.

## Salary And Compensation

- Use recorded compensation preferences when available.
- Do not enter a value below the recorded minimum.
- Do not invent or misrepresent salary history.
- Use ranges or open-ended responses only when the form allows them and recorded policy permits them.
- Pause for equity, bonus, commission, relocation, housing, visa-cost, or benefits tradeoff questions unless policy exists.

## EEO, Disability, Veteran, And Demographics

- Treat these as optional sensitive disclosures.
- Use the user's recorded disclosure preference if present.
- If no preference exists, pause or choose "I do not wish to answer" only when policy authorizes that default.
- Do not infer demographic, disability, veteran, accommodation, gender, race, ethnicity, LGBTQ+, or protected-class answers.
- Do not disclose accommodation needs unless explicitly approved for the application.

## Legal And Consent Questions

- Pause before legal certifications, background-check authorization, arbitration, non-compete acknowledgements, policy agreements, signature fields, or consent to terms that are not purely required site terms for submitting an application.
- Do not agree to assessments, unpaid work, take-home projects, recordings, drug/background checks, or reference checks unless policy or the user authorizes the step.

## Free Responses

- Draft concise, truthful answers grounded in the user's materials.
- Keep the tone direct and specific.
- Do not claim motivations, enthusiasm, availability, preferences, or willingness unless supported by user instructions or recorded policy.
- If a custom answer requires a user-specific story, preference, or claim not already supported, pause with a proposed draft and the missing decision.

## Saved Answer Feedback

Only when the user explicitly requests profile/answer-bank maintenance, record a non-secret normalized version of:

- Question text and answer choices.
- Selected answer and source.
- Confidence and whether user approval was required.
- Any policy-sensitive interpretation.

Do not store raw sensitive values or credentials in saved-answer notes.
